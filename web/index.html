Daily flow at Hope

The table covers the four image dates at the selected offset. Flow is the mean discharge for the gauge day. The percentile rank counts how many of the 30 June daily means were at or below that discharge. Ties count, and the June reference stays fixed when an offset selects a May date. Tides, wind, cloud, illumination and travel time between Hope and the delta affect the appearance. The time offset lets you try your own travel-time assumption. These true-colour images do not measure suspended sediment concentration.
